Kudos Companion

An independent Chrome extension for giving kudos in your Strava browser session, with daily limits and pause controls.

Auto-kudos is free. Pro adds auto-follow and scheduled unfollow for profiles followed by the extension, at 30 SEK per month through ExtensionPay and Stripe. All social actions run through your signed-in Strava session.

Start or pause from one button. Preview actions in dry run, see daily and total counters, and protect individual profiles with Keep.

Pre-release: this extension has not been submitted for Chrome Web Store review. Availability and platform approval are not guaranteed.

Automated access and actions may conflict with Strava’s terms and can lead to account restrictions. Review Strava’s current terms before use. Kudos Companion is not affiliated with or endorsed by Strava.
