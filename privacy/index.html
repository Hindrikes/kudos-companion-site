Kudos Companion privacy policy

Publisher: Aifred AB · Effective 22 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.0.1 and later

Kudos Companion is an independent Chrome extension that automates kudos and, with Pro, following and scheduled unfollowing in your signed-in Strava browser session. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Strava. It does not read or store your Strava password. There is no advertising, analytics, or developer-operated application backend.

Strava data stored on your device

The extension reads relevant Strava page content to find controls, identify the signed-in account, verify actions, read following and follower lists, and detect service warnings. Chrome local extension storage holds settings, daily counters, total counters and their tracking start date, recent action logs, pause and account-mismatch state, extension-owned tab identifiers and timers. It can also hold athlete IDs, names and profile-image URLs, club identifiers, follow queues, follow timestamps and click history, retired follow records, protection lists and follower snapshots. These records support the extension’s visible functions. The popup does not fetch remote athlete profile images.

This Strava data stays in your browser profile. It is not sent to Aifred AB, ExtensionPay, Stripe, or an analytics service. Opening Strava pages and using their controls makes ordinary requests through your existing Strava session. Strava receives those requests and records actions. See Strava’s privacy policy.

Subscription and payment data

Auto-kudos is free. Pro uses ExtensionPay and Stripe. When you choose Upgrade or Manage subscription, ExtensionPay may create a billing identifier. Its bundled SDK stores that identifier, an installation timestamp and returned billing-account information, which can include email, plan and subscription status, in Chrome Sync when available or otherwise Chrome local extension storage. Chrome Sync can synchronize those billing records across your signed-in browser profiles. The extension also locally stores request timing and rate-limit records, and briefly caches entitlement status in Chrome session storage.

The billing identifier is sent to ExtensionPay to verify entitlement and open hosted billing pages. No Strava data is included in billing requests. Checkout and cancellation take place on hosted ExtensionPay/Stripe pages. The extension does not collect or store card numbers. See ExtensionPay’s privacy policy and Stripe’s privacy policy. Uninstalling the extension does not cancel a subscription; use Manage subscription before uninstalling.

Retention, access and deletion

Local settings, follow/protection records, click history and totals remain until normal operation replaces them, you clear the extension’s storage, or you uninstall it. The recent activity log holds at most 300 entries. Daily counters reset by the Europe/Stockholm calendar day; total counters persist and show when tracking began. Earlier activity is not reconstructed. Removing extension storage also removes the history used to distinguish extension-created follows from your personal follows. Back up anything you want to keep before clearing or uninstalling.

You can inspect local records in Chrome’s extension developer tools. Uninstalling clears local extension data. Billing records held by ExtensionPay, Stripe or Chrome Sync are subject to those services’ own retention and deletion processes; contact them to request deletion. Contact Aifred AB for questions about this extension’s handling of your data.

Sharing and security

Aifred AB does not sell extension user data, use it for advertising, unrelated profiling, creditworthiness or lending, or receive copies of Strava records. Network connections use HTTPS. Extension code and the billing SDK are bundled locally; remote executable code is not downloaded. Local records are protected by your operating system and Chrome profile security and are not separately encrypted by the extension.

This website and contact

This policy website uses GitHub Pages and has no analytics, tracking scripts or forms. GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses to serve and secure the site; see GitHub’s privacy statement.
